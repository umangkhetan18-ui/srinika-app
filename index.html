Privacy Policy

Last Updated:

Srinika App is an internal application designed for store managers to manage inventory, sales, and related business operations. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected.

1. Information We Collect

The Srinika App collects only business-related information required for internal store operations, including but not limited to:

  • Product and inventory details
  • Sales and billing information
  • Store user login credentials (provided by the store owner)

2. How We Use Information

The information collected is used solely to:

  • Manage inventory and stock levels
  • Record and track sales transactions
  • Generate internal reports
  • Improve app functionality and performance

3. Data Sharing

Srinika App does not sell, share, or disclose any data to third parties. All data is strictly used for internal business purposes of the store.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information stored within the app. Access to the app is restricted to authorized users only.

5. User Responsibility

The store owner or manager is responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of login credentials and ensuring appropriate use of the application.

6. Children's Privacy

Srinika App is intended for business use only and is not designed for children under the age of 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the app administrator or store owner.
